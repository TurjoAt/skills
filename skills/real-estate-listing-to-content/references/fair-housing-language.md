# U.S. fair housing language

Describe the dwelling, terms, and verified amenities. Federal housing advertising rules cover preferences or limitations based on race, color, religion, sex, disability, familial status, and national origin. Check state and local protections as well, including any applicable rules on age, source of income, marital status, sexual orientation, or gender identity. The examples below show editorial rewrites. Use only substitutes that the listing record supports. [24 CFR § 100.75](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title24-vol1/xhtml/CFR-2025-title24-vol1-sec100-75.html)

## Pre-publish review

- [ ] Read the headline, body, FAQ, CTA, hashtags, image overlays, captions, and alt text for expressed or implied occupant preferences.
- [ ] Confirm property features, measurements, distances, and terms against approved records.
- [ ] Describe accessibility features using measurements or observable conditions. Route legal compliance claims for review.
- [ ] Check eligibility, occupancy, age restrictions, accommodation language, and disclosures with the publisher for the applicable jurisdiction.
- [ ] Review any paid targeting, exclusions, audience criteria, and placement for housing advertising requirements and platform controls.
- [ ] Apply current MLS and brokerage requirements and record the publisher's approval before release.
