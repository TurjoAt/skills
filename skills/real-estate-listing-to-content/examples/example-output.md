# Springfield home for sale at 128 Maple Grove Lane

128 Maple Grove Lane is a Springfield single-family home listed for $425,000. It has three bedrooms, two full bathrooms, and 1,680 square feet of interior space according to the listing record. One bedroom and one full bathroom are on the main level, with the other two bedrooms and the second bathroom upstairs. The dining area opens onto a covered rear patio and a fenced backyard. If you're deciding which homes to tour, start by looking at how the rooms connect on each level and where the outdoor door sits in relation to the dining area.

## Main-level layout

The living room and dining area connect directly. The dining-area photo looks toward the patio doors, which makes it a useful starting point for understanding how the indoor rooms relate to the backyard. On a tour, start in the living room, continue into the dining area, and step out onto the patio. That route makes it easier to picture where you would put a dining table and how you would keep the patio doors clear when the table is in use.

The main-level bedroom gives you another room on the same floor as the living and dining areas. A full bathroom is also on this level. If you have a specific furniture arrangement in mind, bring measurements for the pieces that matter most and check wall lengths, door swings, and clearance at the bedroom door during the visit. The total interior area gives a sense of scale for the house, and an in-person measurement can answer questions about an individual room.

## Kitchen and laundry

The kitchen has a prep island with seating for two and a window above the sink. The island provides another work surface within the kitchen. In the kitchen photograph, look for the relationship between the seating side of the island and the sink. At the showing, stand near the island and check how much room you would have to move around it with both seats in use. If you're bringing stools, check seat height and width against the actual counter and seating area.

Laundry is off the kitchen. Include it in the main-level walkthrough so you can see the route between the laundry area and the staircase. If appliance fit is important, bring the dimensions of the units you plan to use and ask the listing agent what is included in the sale. Check the dimensions of the laundry area and its doorway before making an appliance decision.

## Upstairs bedrooms and bath

The two upper-level bedrooms face different directions. The front bedroom has two street-facing windows, and the rear bedroom overlooks the backyard. During a visit, check each room from the doorway and from the windows. Those two viewpoints can help you judge where you would place a bed or desk and how much of the window area you want to leave clear.

The upstairs full bathroom opens off the hall. That location gives both upstairs bedrooms a route to it from the hallway. Walk the route from each bedroom during the showing and use the time upstairs to check any room dimensions or window details that matter to your plans.

## Patio and parking

The dining area opens directly to the covered rear patio. Beyond it is a fenced yard with a planted border along the rear fence. The patio photo shows the covered area and the border in the same view. If you plan to set out a table or chairs, measure the space you want to furnish and leave room for the path from the dining area to the yard. Ask about the fence or planted border if ownership or maintenance affects your plans.

The driveway fits two cars in tandem. With both spaces occupied, the vehicle nearer the street may need to move first. At the showing, compare the driveway with the lengths of the vehicles you use and check how you would enter and leave when both are parked.
